01 · The Artist

Four sounds, one continuous language.

CRISELVC is an electronic artist working across tech house, Latin house, Afro house, and progressive — four sounds she treats as one continuous language rather than four separate sets.

Formerly performing as DJ Crisel (DJL), she rebuilt the project around a single idea: sound without borders.

Her sets don’t switch between genres; they move through them — holding a room in one unbroken form from the first groove to the final lift.

02 · Statement

I was never from one place.
Neither is my sound.

“Born in Dubai, Filipino by blood, trained in Ibiza. I grew up in rooms where nobody shared a first language and everybody still danced. So Latin heat, Afro pulse, tech house drive and progressive lift were never four genres to me — they’re the accents of the same room I was raised in.”

“The percussion in my hands is how I say it without picking a language. I don’t blend cultures to make a point. I play the way I grew up: everything at once, nothing translated, no border where one ends and the next begins.”

— CriselVC

The Story

The move to CRISELVC was not cosmetic.

As DJ Crisel, later DJL, she spent years learning the unglamorous craft of the working DJ: reading unfamiliar rooms, holding a floor when the energy dipped, and figuring out what actually moves people rather than what looks good in a set list.

Her sound sits where the Gulf’s club culture meets an international dance-floor sensibility — the UAE club and lifestyle circuit, resort stages across the Maldives, and a touring history reaching into Europe and Southeast Asia.

What distinguishes her is control — less the individual track than the architecture of the whole set: where energy rises, where it breathes, how a room is guided from arrival to peak to release. Sound without borders is the thesis; the sets are the proof.

04 · On the Floor

A room that feels like a single wave.

A CRISELVC set is built as one movement. She opens by settling the room — groove-forward, unhurried — then tightens it. The peak is rhythmic and physical; the release leans progressive — depth and lift rather than a hard stop.
